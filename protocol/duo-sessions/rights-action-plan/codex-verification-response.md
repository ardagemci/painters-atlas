Checked **7 September 2026**. These are the organizations’ **asserted licensing mandates**, with Pigment-specific applicability unresolved.

| Artist | Verified licensing route |
|---|---|
| **Picasso** | **Picasso Administration**, acting for Succession Picasso; **ARS** represents the estate in the US. [Administration](https://picasso.fr/les-droits/), [ARS](https://arsny.com/history/) |
| **Kahlo** | **Banco de México Diego Rivera–Frida Kahlo Museums Trust**, through **ARS** in the US. **Frida Kahlo Corporation** advertises brand/trademark licensing; its website does not establish authority over painting reproductions. [ARS credits](https://arsny.com/image-credits/), [Corporation](https://www.fridakahlocorporation.com/) |
| **Warhol** | **Andy Warhol Foundation** directs reproduction requests to **ARS** for US/rest-of-world, **DACS** for UK, and **ADAGP** for France/Monaco/Luxembourg. [Foundation](https://warholfoundation.org/warhol/licensing/) |
| **Pollock** | **Pollock-Krasner Foundation** explicitly directs all artwork reproduction/licensing requests to **ARS**. [Foundation](https://www.pkf.org/about/resources/) |
| **Rothko** | **Kate Rothko Prizel and Christopher Rothko**, represented by **ARS** in the US. [ARS credits](https://arsny.com/image-credits/) |
| **Dalí** | **Gala-Salvador Dalí Foundation** directs artwork reproduction requests to **VEGAP**, Spain, operating through the international collecting-society network. [Foundation](https://www.salvador-dali.org/en/dali-foundation/services/rights-and-image/) |

**Web/editorial evidence:** Warhol explicitly lists websites; ARS markets digital news/editorial and scholarly licensing. This establishes a standard licensing channel, but does not establish routine approval of nonprofit art-atlas requests. [ARS editorial services](https://arsny.com/license-for-media/)

**Deal structure:** DACS says charges are generally **per artwork and per use**, while also documenting annual blanket agreements for regular users. [Pricing practice](https://www.dacs.org.uk/licensing/what-is-licensing/costs-for-licensing), [blanket agreements](https://cdn.dacs.org.uk/uploads/documents/DACS-Code-of-Conduct-licensing-customers.pdf?v=1762429108)

**Pricing:** I found no comparable public estate/ARS tariff for Pigment’s use. UK DACS does publish museum digital rates: **£10/work/year for 10–100 collection/loan artworks**, excluding VAT. [Published tariff](https://www.dacs.org.uk/licensing/what-is-licensing/licensing-costs-for-public-museums) I don’t know whether Pigment qualifies; artist coverage, territories, duration, and separate photograph permissions remain unverified.
