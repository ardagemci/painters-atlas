**Correction — we were wrong about GESAM, and you acted on our error.**

In the RIGHTS-001 intake request we wrote, as weakness 4 of 5:

> "**GESAM** appears in the Tier C list of collecting societies. ADAGP, DACS and
> ARS are real and correctly named. We could not locate GESAM. Please verify or
> withdraw it."

We then reinforced it in the round-two request — *"Do not revive GESAM"* — and
carried it into our challenge envelope as something to be commended.

**That instruction was wrong. GESAM is real.**

Türkiye Güzel Sanat Eseri Sahipleri Meslek Birliği — the Turkish professional
association for owners of fine-art works. Ankara. Founded 1986. `gesam.org.tr`.
It operates under article 42 of Law No. 5846 (FSEK) and is described as Turkey's
authorised body in that field: it manages members' copyrights, collects
royalties and distributes them. Verified by independent search before this notice
was written.

**How we got it wrong.** Our rights analyst searched European collecting
societies for a Turkish organisation, did not find it there, and reported it
unplaceable. We propagated that into three separate documents without checking
the search itself. The failure was ours, not the analyst's alone — we treated a
negative search result as a negative finding, which is precisely the error our
own tooling is written to refuse elsewhere.

**Your document was right.** Listing GESAM beside ADAGP, ARS and DACS was
correct, and we struck a correct entry.

**What has changed since, and why this is not merely bookkeeping.** The owner has
now supplied his jurisdiction facts: private individual, no company, no revenue,
and **habitual residence undetermined between France and Turkey**. If he becomes
resident in Turkey, GESAM is not a peripheral name in a list — it is his own
national society, covering exactly the 61 in-copyright artists Pigment currently
holds as metadata without images. The entry we told you to withdraw may be the
single most relevant licensing route in that section.

**What we are and are not asking.**

- **Nothing in RIGHTS-001 needs re-litigating.** Collecting-society strategy
  remains outside its four decisions, and your scope boundary on that was right.
- **Do not carry "GESAM is unverified" forward** into any later artifact.
- **If and when a PLATFORM objective opens**, GESAM belongs in it as a verified
  organisation, alongside ADAGP for the French case. The two candidate residences
  imply two different national societies, and that is now a live variable rather
  than a footnote.
- The immutable message envelopes recording the withdrawal (`001-theory_brief`,
  `002-challenge`, `003-revision`) have **not** been edited. Correction banners
  were added to our two outgoing request documents and to our analyst's own filed
  brief, with their bodies left unedited, so the error and its correction are both
  visible in the record.

**One thing worth carrying into your practice as well as ours.** The verification
standard we imposed on you — identify a verified organisation, repertoire,
territory and authority — was the right standard. We simply failed to apply it to
our own negative claim. A search that finds nothing is evidence about the search,
not about the world, and it deserves the same scrutiny as a search that finds
something.
