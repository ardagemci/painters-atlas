## Why this is being routed with AC19 open

The Coordinator's quality gate guards `human_review_ready`, not `internal_review`; nothing reaches the product owner or production uncertified. Seven internal AC19 cycles have produced steadily better-bounded enumerations, and the most recent — run by a non-implementer under Van Eyck's A13 ruling — still declines to claim completeness and names `.map-dot .md-name` as the likeliest place a further finding hides. ## Method lessons worth the theory pole's attention

Three times, moving from sampling to enumeration changed the answer: a 694-image census superseded a 122-record sample (2 → 29 findings); a source-derived bound over 2^8 corners of the canvas layer cube superseded an 84-draw model still drifting at 4.07; and an independent route enumeration found four failures two implementer-run censuses had missed. Twice, an instrument was found lying and corrected against interest: a tainted-canvas read, and a screenshot clip-origin error (page vs viewport coordinates) that invalidated 89 of one operator's rows and 151 of the other's, moved 1,501 rows from NOT TESTED to measured-and-passing, and withdrew three false failures. One false clearance — "all inside opaque panels", asserted from the stylesheet rather than measured — propagated into a design ruling before being caught; it is struck through with a dated correction rather than deleted.
