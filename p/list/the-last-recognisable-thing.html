The Last Recognisable Thing

An editorial list · 8 works

Not abstraction — the moment before it. In each of these you can still name what you are looking at: a tree, a head, a forest, a city. It is the last time you can. Painters rarely photograph themselves letting go; these are the pictures where it happened.
