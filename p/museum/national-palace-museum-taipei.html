National Palace Museum

Taipei · Taiwan · The imperial collection that left the mainland in crates

Photograph: Jason Zhang · CC0 · file on Commons

Nearly seven hundred thousand objects from the Chinese imperial collection, moved out of Beijing ahead of the Japanese advance in 1933, carried around the country through a war, and shipped to Taiwan in 1948 and 1949. Almost nothing was lost. It is the strangest provenance story in the museum world and it is why the greatest surviving Song paintings are on an island.
