Topkapı Palace Museum

Istanbul · Turkey · Four courtyards, four centuries of sultans, one nakkaşhane

Photograph: Sahar.Ahmed · CC BY-SA 4.0 · file on Commons

Mehmed II began it in the 1460s, a decade after taking Constantinople, and the Ottoman court ran from it for close to four hundred years — not one palace but a walled city of courtyards, kitchens, treasuries and a harem, each gate admitting fewer people than the last. It became a museum in 1924, by order of the new republic.
