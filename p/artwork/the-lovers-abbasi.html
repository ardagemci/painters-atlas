The Lovers

Reza Abbasi · 1630 · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Two figures fold into one another on a page you could hold in one hand. Nothing happens but the touching — no depth, no room, no story arriving. Reza Abbasi worked inside Safavid album convention at the height of it, and the whole picture is about how a sleeve sits against a shoulder. The sheet carries its own date: 8 Shawwal 1039, which is 21 May 1630.

  • The sheet is dated 8 Shawwal 1039 — 21 May 1630
  • No depth is attempted; the idiom never wanted it
  • The real subject is the fall of cloth
