The Cheat with the Ace of Diamonds

Georges de La Tour · c. 1635 · Musée du Louvre, Paris · 106 × 146 cm

Four people at a table, and three of them are in on it. The cheat pulls an ace from his belt behind his back while the courtesan and the servant exchange a look over the wine; the rich boy on the right studies his own hand and sees none of it. Every glance in the picture goes somewhere except his. La Tour was forgotten for two and a half centuries and only rediscovered in 1915.

  • Three people are looking at each other; the mark is not
  • The ace comes from his belt, behind his back
  • La Tour vanished from art history until 1915
