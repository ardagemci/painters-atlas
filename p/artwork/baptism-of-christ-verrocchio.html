The Baptism of Christ

Andrea del Verrocchio · c. 1475 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

A workshop picture, and you can see the seam. The angel on the far left — the one turned away, with the soft hair and the landscape dissolving behind it — was painted by an apprentice named Leonardo, in oil, while the rest is his master's tempera. Vasari says Verrocchio looked at that corner and never painted again. He did, in fact. But the story survives because the corner really is different.

  • The left-hand angel is Leonardo's, and it is painted in oil
  • The rest is tempera by the master — the two surfaces disagree
  • Vasari's tale of a teacher quitting in despair is a good story
