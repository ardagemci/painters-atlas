Landscape with the Fall of Icarus

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · c. 1560 · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, Brussels · 73.5 × 112 cm

A ploughman works, a shepherd stares up at nothing in particular, a ship sails on — and in the bottom right corner two legs disappear into the water. That is Icarus. The proportion is the entire argument: the myth takes up about one per cent of the picture and not one person in it has noticed. Whether Bruegel painted this actual panel is now disputed.

  • Icarus is two legs, bottom right, already going under
