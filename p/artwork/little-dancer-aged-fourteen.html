Little Dancer Aged Fourteen

Edgar Degas · 1878–81 · Philadelphia Museum of Art, Philadelphia · 98 cm (bronze cast; wax original in Washington)

Image credit: Regan Vercruysse from Stewartsville, New Jersey, USA · CC BY 2.0 · file on Commons

Degas showed the wax original in 1881 dressed in a real bodice, tutu and slippers, with human hair and a satin ribbon, standing inside a glass case like a specimen. Critics were appalled: they called her a monkey and read her face as evidence of criminality. She was Marie van Goethem, fourteen, a dancer at the Paris Opéra. The bronzes were cast after his death — he never sold one.
