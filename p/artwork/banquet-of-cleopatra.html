The Banquet of Cleopatra

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo · 1743–44 · National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne

Cleopatra has bet Antony she can spend a fortune on a single meal, and she is about to win by dropping a pearl into vinegar and drinking it. Tiepolo stages the wager on a checkerboard floor under a sky that opens straight out of the architecture, which is what he did better than anyone alive. The picture went from Venice to Dresden to Catherine the Great, and was sold out of Russia in 1932.

  • She is dropping the pearl; the wager is about to be settled
  • The sky opens directly out of the colonnade — his signature move
  • Owned by Catherine the Great, sold by the Soviet government in 1932
