The Concert

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1664 · Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, Boston · 72.5 × 64.7 cm

Three musicians at a harpsichord, with two paintings on the wall behind them — one a Baburen brothel scene that Vermeer owned and used more than once. Nobody has seen this picture since 18 March 1990, when two men in police uniform talked their way into the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum and left with it and twelve other works. It is the most valuable stolen painting in the world.

  • Stolen from the Gardner Museum on 18 March 1990
  • Its empty frame still hangs where the painting was
  • A Baburen brothel scene hangs behind the players
