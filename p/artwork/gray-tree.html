Gray Tree

Piet Mondrian · 1911 · Kunstmuseum Den Haag, The Hague · 79.7 × 109.1 cm

This is the hinge. Mondrian had been painting trees for years; here the branches stop describing a tree and start behaving like structure, and the background pushes forward to meet them until you cannot say which is in front. The palette has come down to greys and blue-greys and almost nothing else. The tree's mass is faintly oval — within a few years that oval becomes an explicit frame, and within ten there is nothing left but straight lines and three colours.

  • Branch and background are painted at the same depth
  • Its mass is faintly oval — that oval later becomes his frame
  • He had painted the same subject red a few years before
