Still Life with Cheeses, Almonds and Pretzels

Clara Peeters · c. 1615 · Mauritshuis, The Hague

Three cheeses stacked like masonry, a knife balanced on the edge of the table, pretzels, almonds, butter, a glass of wine. Peeters painted food as though it had weight, which in 1615 was new. Look into the pewter lid and the goblet: there are minute reflections of a woman at an easel, which is the closest thing to a biography she left. Almost nothing else about her life is documented.

  • Her reflection hides in the metal — check the pewter lid
  • The cheeses are stacked, not arranged: this is a tower
  • Beyond a handful of signed panels, her life is undocumented
