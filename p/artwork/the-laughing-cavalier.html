The Laughing Cavalier

Frans Hals · 1624 · The Wallace Collection, London · 83 × 67.3 cm

He is not laughing, and nobody knows he was a cavalier. The title was attached in the nineteenth century and stuck. What is actually here is the moustache, the tilt, and the sleeve — an acre of embroidery loaded with bees, arrows and lovers' knots, painted wet into wet at speed while the face was worked slowly. Hals lets you see every stroke, three hundred years before that was a virtue.
