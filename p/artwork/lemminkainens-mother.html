Lemminkäinen's Mother

Akseli Gallen-Kallela · 1897 · Ateneum Art Museum, Helsinki · 85.5 × 108.5 cm

A mother kneels on the bank of the river of the dead and puts her dismembered son back together. Gallen-Kallela painted it in 1897 in tempera rather than salon oil — flattened, hard-edged, bounded like decoration — so that a scene of that kind holds absolutely still. The stillness is not calm. Nothing in the picture moves and everything in it is at pitch.

  • Tempera, not oil — a refusal of salon finish
  • The body is being assembled, not mourned
  • Motionless, and not remotely calm
