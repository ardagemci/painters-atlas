The Raft of the Medusa

Théodore Géricault · 1818–1819 · Musée du Louvre, Paris · 491 × 716 cm

Seven metres of canvas — the size the Salon reserved for scripture and antiquity — given over to a news story. Bodies pile into a pyramid, the dead at the base and the living straining up out of it, and at the apex there is no hero, no state and no moral resolution: only a figure signalling at a speck on the horizon.

  • Seven metres wide, at Salon scale, for contemporary news
  • The apex holds no hero and no state
  • A speck on the horizon is the whole plot
