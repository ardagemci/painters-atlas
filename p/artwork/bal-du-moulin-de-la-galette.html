Bal du moulin de la Galette

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1876 · Musée d'Orsay, Paris · 131.5 × 176.5 cm

A Sunday afternoon at the Moulin de la Galette in Montmartre, where working Parisians dressed up to dance, drink and eat galettes into the evening. The sun comes through the trees in loose violet patches and is allowed to fall across faces, which is the Impressionist argument in one gesture. Gustave Caillebotte owned it; when he died the French state took it in lieu of death duties. And Renoir painted the scene twice.
