Marriage A-la-Mode

William Hogarth · 1743–45 · The National Gallery, London

The opening scene of six: a bankrupt earl trades his son to a rich merchant's daughter, and everybody in the room is looking at something other than the couple. He points at his family tree; the merchant reads the contract; the two young people sit back to back, one admiring himself, the other twisting her handkerchief. Two dogs are chained together in the corner, in case the point were missed.

  • The bride and groom face away from each other from the very start
  • The earl points to a family tree; the merchant reads the money
  • Two dogs chained together sit in the corner, bottom left
