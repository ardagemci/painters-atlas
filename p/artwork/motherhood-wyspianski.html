Motherhood

Stanisław Wyspiański · 1905 · National Museum in Kraków, Kraków

Wyspiański drew his wife nursing, in pastel, without softening anything: she is tired, she is not looking at the child, and the chalk is left visibly on the paper. He made the same subject repeatedly across the 1900s and this is the one people mean. He was a playwright and a stained-glass designer as much as a painter, and the outline here is a glazier's — one continuous line doing all the work.

  • A glazier's outline: one continuous line holds it
  • She is exhausted, and he did not tidy that away
  • Pastel, left visibly chalky rather than blended
