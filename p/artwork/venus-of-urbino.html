Venus of Urbino

Titian · 1538 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence · 119 × 165.5 cm

A woman on a bed in an ordinary sixteenth-century bedroom, looking at you, and looking as though she has been for some time. Two maids at a chest in the background, a dog asleep, white linen and fur. Nothing here is mythological except the word Venus in the title. The reclining nude that Western painting used for the next three hundred years starts about here.

  • She met your eye before you arrived
  • The only mythology is in the title
  • Two maids at a chest; a dog asleep
