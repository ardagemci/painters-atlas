The Piano Lesson

Henri Matisse · 1916 · The Museum of Modern Art, New York

His son Pierre at the keyboard, reduced to a wedge of grey with a scored triangle of shadow across one eye. This is Matisse at his most severe: wartime, Cubist-adjacent, colour rationed to grey-green and one slice of garden. Two of his own works stand guard — a painted woman perched high at the right, a bronze nude reclining at the left. The metronome ticks in the middle. Practice, watched by art, in a house where the war goes unmentioned.

  • The grey triangle across Pierre's eye — a shadow with a ruler's edge
  • Top right is Matisse's own painting; bottom left, his own sculpture
  • Green enters once, through the window, and nowhere else
