Rucellai Madonna

Duccio di Buoninsegna · 1285 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

The largest panel painted in Italy in its century, ordered by a lay confraternity for their chapel in Santa Maria Novella. Six angels kneel on the frame rather than in the space, holding the throne up like porters. Duccio was Sienese and the commission was Florentine, and for centuries Florence preferred to believe Cimabue had painted it. The tenderness gives him away: the cloth falls in curves nobody in Florence was drawing yet.

  • The angels kneel on the frame itself, not in the picture
  • Florence attributed it to Cimabue for roughly six hundred years
  • Ordered by a confraternity of laymen, not by the church
