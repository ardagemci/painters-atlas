Madonna with the Long Neck

Parmigianino · 1534–40 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

Everything is stretched — the neck, the fingers, the thigh under the silk, the enormous child asleep across her lap like a body being carried. On the right a row of columns supports nothing and stops in mid-air, and a prophet the size of a thumb stands at their base unrolling a scroll. Parmigianino never finished it and died at thirty-seven. Beauty pushed until it goes wrong is exactly the point.

  • The colonnade on the right holds nothing up and simply stops
  • The prophet at its foot is painted at a fraction of proper scale
  • Unfinished: the panel was still in the studio when he died
