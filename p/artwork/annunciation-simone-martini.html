Annunciation with Saints Margaret and Ansanus

Simone Martini · 1333 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

Gabriel has just spoken and the words run out of his mouth in raised gold letters, straight across the panel to Mary — who pulls away, gathers her cloak, and looks as though she would rather not. That recoil is the whole picture. Painted for Siena cathedral, where the lily that belongs in this scene was a Florentine emblem, so the olive branch stands in for it. Diplomacy, in gold leaf.

  • The angel's words are raised gold, crossing the panel as an object
  • Mary pulls back and gathers her cloak — she is not pleased
  • An olive branch replaces the lily, which was Florence's flower
