The Tortoise Trainer

Osman Hamdi Bey · 1906 · Pera Museum, Istanbul · 221.5 × 120 cm

A man in Ottoman dress stands stooped over his tortoises, a naqareh drum slung on his back, waiting for them to learn something. They do not hurry. Nothing in the picture moves at all. Osman Hamdi Bey built it to be read rather than felt, and gave two and a quarter metres of canvas to the slowest lesson in the room.

  • The drum on his back is a naqareh
  • Nothing in this picture is in a hurry
  • That the trainer wears the painter's own face — widely repeated, unestablished
