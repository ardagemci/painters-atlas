The Holy Trinity

Masaccio · 1425–1426 · Basilica di Santa Maria Novella, Florence · 667 × 317 cm

The architecture behind the figures is built in one-point perspective, and the vanishing point sits at the eye level of someone standing in the church — so the painted space is not a backdrop, it is the room you are in. A painted tomb and its inscription run beneath. Masaccio put this on a wall around 1425, and afterwards the tradition had to use it or refuse it.
