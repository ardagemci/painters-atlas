Self-Portrait Holding a Portrait of Her Sister

Rosalba Carriera · 1715 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

She looks up from her work holding a portrait of her sister Giovanna, who was also her assistant, and the whole picture is chalk. Carriera took pastel — a medium for sketches — and made it a finished art that half of Europe then wanted, travelling to Paris and Vienna to supply it. The softness is the argument: skin rendered in dust, fixed to paper. She went blind before she died, still in Venice.

  • Entirely in pastel, which she raised from sketching tool to art
  • The portrait she holds is her sister and studio assistant, Giovanna
  • Paris sat for her in crowds; she died blind, back home in Venice
