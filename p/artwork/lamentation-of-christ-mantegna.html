Lamentation of Christ

Andrea Mantegna · c. 1470–1474 · Pinacoteca di Brera, Milan · 68 × 81 cm

The body is laid out feet-first, straight at you, in the hardest thing perspective can be asked to do. Then Mantegna adjusts it: the proportions are visibly tuned so that the feet do not swallow the head. Three mourners weep beside him. It is a technical demonstration wearing the clothes of a devotional picture, and the whole thing is 68 centimetres wide.

  • Feet-first foreshortening, the hardest problem perspective has
  • The proportions are tuned so feet don't dominate
  • 68 centimetres — narrower than a briefcase is wide
