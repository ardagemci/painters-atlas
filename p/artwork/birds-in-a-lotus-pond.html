Birds in a Lotus Pond

Bada Shanren · 1690 · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

One perched bird, made of two strokes and a dot for an eye, on a great deal of empty paper. Bada Shanren painted inside the literati tradition and at its far edge — the reduction was his, not the convention's. The mood is not serene. It is sour, and the bird looks as though it has formed an opinion about you.

  • The eye is a single dot, and it judges
  • Most of the sheet is left as paper
  • The 1690 date rests on one source
