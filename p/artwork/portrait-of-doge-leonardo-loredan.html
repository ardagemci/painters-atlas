Portrait of Doge Leonardo Loredan

Giovanni Bellini · c. 1501–1502 · The National Gallery, London · 61.6 × 45.1 cm

Loredan had just been elected Doge and would hold the office for twenty years. Bellini painted him almost square-on, which portraiture of the time avoided, and painted the office rather than the mood: the corno ducale over its linen cap, the damask robe, the buttons done in thick raised paint so they catch real light. The signature is on a painted scrap of paper along the ledge. It says only IOANNES BELLINVS.

  • The buttons are raised paint, and catch the room's light
  • The hat is a doublet's hood, stiffened into a crown
  • Signed on a painted slip of paper on the ledge
