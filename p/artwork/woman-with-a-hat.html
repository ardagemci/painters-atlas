Woman with a Hat

Henri Matisse · 1905 · San Francisco Museum of Modern Art, San Francisco

His wife Amélie, in a black dress — Matisse said so himself when asked, though nothing here is black. The hat is a landslide of orange and violet; a green shadow divides the face for no reason the eye can justify. Hung at the 1905 Salon d'Automne in the room a critic nicknamed the cage of wild beasts, it was laughed at and scratched at. Gertrude and Leo Stein bought it anyway, and Fauvism had its first patron.

  • The green stripe down her face — the shadow that started the scandal
  • Matisse insisted the dress was black; the paint says every colour but
  • Bare canvas shows through at the edges: finished, and left unfinished
