Santa Trinita Maestà

Cimabue · c. 1290–1300 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

Gold, and a throne built like a piece of architecture. Eight angels stack up its sides rather than standing around it, and four prophets watch from the arcade underneath, each holding a scroll that argues the case for the Incarnation. Cimabue worked before painting learned perspective and gets depth anyway by carpentry: the throne recedes because it is drawn as a building. The gold is not a background. It is the light.

  • Eight angels are stacked, not spaced — the throne is a structure
  • Four prophets sit in the arcade below, holding their scrolls of proof
  • No perspective yet: the depth is joinery, done in gold
