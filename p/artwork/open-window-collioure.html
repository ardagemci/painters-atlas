Open Window, Collioure

Henri Matisse · 1905 · National Gallery of Art, Washington, D.C.

Summer in a Catalan fishing port, painted with the shutters thrown open and the rules thrown out. Boats ride a harbour of pink and turquoise; the wall on the left is green, the wall on the right violet, and nothing explains it except that the pairing sings. Inside and outside sit on one flat plane, the window frame doing the work perspective used to do. Matisse painted windows for another fifty years. This is the one that opened.

  • Interior and exterior share a single flat surface — intervals, not depth
  • The two walls are different colours; the picture never justifies it
  • Painted at Collioure with Derain, the summer Fauvism was invented
