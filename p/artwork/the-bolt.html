The Bolt

Jean-Honoré Fragonard · 1777 · Musée du Louvre, Paris · 73.5 × 93.5 cm

Everything is thrown along one diagonal: a red bed curtain, a wrecked gold bedspread, two bodies, and at the top corner a hand driving the bolt home. What is happening in it is disputed and has been for a long time. One reading is two lovers shutting the door; another is a woman trying to get free while a man locks it. Her arm is straight and her face is turned away. The picture does not settle it.

  • A single diagonal runs from the bed to the bolt
  • Her arm is braced against him, not around him
  • Scholars read this as an embrace or as an assault
