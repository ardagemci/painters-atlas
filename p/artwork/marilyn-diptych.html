Marilyn Diptych

Andy Warhol · 1962 · Tate Modern, London

Fifty identical Marilyns silkscreened from a single 1953 publicity still: twenty-five in colour on the left panel, twenty-five in black and white on the right, smearing and fading across the surface until the last of them are barely there at all. She had died a few weeks before he made it. The repetition is the point, and so is the fading.
