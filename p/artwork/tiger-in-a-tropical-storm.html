Tiger in a Tropical Storm (Surprised!)

Henri Rousseau · 1891 · The National Gallery, London · 129.8 × 161.9 cm

His first jungle, and the storm is doing most of the work: silver rain drawn in diagonal lines straight across the canvas, grasses bent flat, and a tiger caught mid-pounce with its eyes wide. Critics laughed at the flat leaves and the toy-like beast. The bent grass and the rain are, in fact, exactly observed — he had watched storms, if not tigers.

  • His first jungle painting, and the critics laughed

  • Nobody has agreed what surprised the tiger
