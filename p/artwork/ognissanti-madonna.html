Ognissanti Madonna

Giotto di Bondone · c. 1300–1305 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence · 325 × 204 cm

Gold ground, angels ranked by importance, everything the convention asked for — and then a knee pushes out under the drapery and the whole thing changes. Giotto kept the hierarchy of a Byzantine Maestà and threw the flatness away. The throne is built in depth. The body has weight. Three and a quarter metres of panel, made to be met across a church.

  • A knee carries real weight under the drapery
  • The throne is built in depth, not stacked
  • Gold ground kept; the flatness thrown out
