The Fate of the Animals

Franz Marc · 1913 · Kunstmuseum Basel, Basel · 195 × 263.5 cm

Marc's own title was different: the trees showed their rings, the animals their veins. Red wedges cut a forest apart, a blue deer throws its head back at the centre, and two horses run the wrong way — into it. He painted this in 1913. In 1915, at the front, a postcard of it reached him, and he wrote to his wife that it looked like a premonition of the war. He was killed at Verdun the next year.

  • The right third is browner: it burned, and Klee repainted it
  • Klee kept the repair visible rather than faking the colour
