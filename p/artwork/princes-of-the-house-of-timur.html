Princes of the House of Timur

Abd al-Samad · c. 1550–1555 · The British Museum, London

A garden pavilion with the Timurid dynasty seated in it, painted on cotton rather than paper and at a size no page could hold. Large areas are simply gone — the losses are part of what you see. It was begun around 1550, probably at Kabul, and repainted repeatedly over the following century as the faces of later emperors were added to their ancestors. A dynasty updating its own family photograph.

  • Painted on cloth, not paper, and much of it is lost
  • Later emperors were painted in among their ancestors
  • One of the earliest things the Mughal workshop made
