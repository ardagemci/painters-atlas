Self-Portrait at the Age of 63

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1669 · The National Gallery, London · 86 × 70.5 cm

Painted in the year he died: bankrupt, having outlived his wife, his companion and his son. The hands are folded and barely finished. The face is worked and reworked until the paint has the texture of skin that has been lived in, and he looks straight out with no expression you can put a name to. He is not asking the viewer for anything at all.

  • Painted in 1669, the year Rembrandt died
  • The hands are barely finished; the face is not
  • One of roughly eighty self-portraits over forty years
