The Heart of the Andes

Frederic Edwin Church · 1859 · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York · 168 × 302.9 cm

No such place exists. Church spent two journeys in Ecuador and Colombia retracing Alexander von Humboldt's route, then assembled a continent — Chimborazo, a plain, a jungle — into one three-metre view. In New York in 1859 people queued and paid twenty-five cents to see it, in a darkened room, lit theatrically, in a floor-standing frame built to look like a window. He meant to ship it to Humboldt. Humboldt died that May.
