The Resurrection

Piero della Francesca · c. 1463–65 · Museo Civico, Sansepolcro

Christ steps out of the tomb with one foot on its edge and stares straight out, while four soldiers sleep underneath in a heap of armour. Look at the trees: bare on the left, in leaf on the right, so the landscape performs the resurrection as well. Piero painted it for his own town hall. In 1944 a British officer shelling the town recalled reading that the best picture in the world was here, and stopped.

  • The trees are winter on the left and summer on the right
  • Christ's foot rests on the lip of the tomb, halfway out
  • A 1944 bombardment was called off because of this painting
