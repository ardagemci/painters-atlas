The Battle of San Romano

Paolo Uccello · c. 1438–40 · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

One of three panels made for a Florentine bedroom, and this is the middle of the fight: a commander going backwards off a white horse, lances crossing overhead like a roof. Uccello laid the broken weapons on the ground in a deliberate grid because perspective was the thing he actually cared about. Behind the carnage, on a brown hillside, hares run from dogs. Vasari said his wife complained he would not come to bed.

  • The broken lances on the ground are laid out as a perspective grid
  • Behind the battle the hillside carries a small hunt, hares and all
  • One of three panels; the others are in London and Paris
