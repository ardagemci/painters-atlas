Frescoes of the Church of the Transfiguration, Novgorod

Theophanes the Greek · 1378 · Church of the Transfiguration on Ilyina Street, Veliky Novgorod

A Byzantine painter walked into a Russian town and painted a church in a single season, and 1378 is not an estimate — a chronicle wrote it down. What survives is fragments: Christ in the dome, saints by the south door, the Old Testament Trinity in the west chamber. This picture is one of them, a martyr's head. Look at the whites: two or three flicked strokes make a cheekbone. Nobody in Russia was painting like that.
