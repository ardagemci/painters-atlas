The Ambassadors

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1533 · The National Gallery, London · 207 × 209.5 cm

Two young Frenchmen in London in 1533, with a shelf of instruments between them: globes, a sundial, a lute, a hymn book. Every object is a claim about knowledge, and several of them are wrong on purpose — the lute has a broken string. Then there is the grey smear across the floor. Stand at the right edge and look back along the picture and it resolves into a skull. You cannot see the skull and the men at the same time.

  • The grey smear is a skull, readable only from the side
  • A lute string is broken — discord, deliberately placed
  • The instruments are set to a real date: 11 April 1533
