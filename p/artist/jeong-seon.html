Jeong Seon

1676–1759 · He stopped painting an imaginary China and went to look at Korea

Known by the art name Gyeomjae, 'humble study', which was accurate about his position rather than his talent: he depended on aristocratic patrons and the name acknowledged it.

For three centuries Joseon painters had made landscapes of an idealised China most of them had never seen.
