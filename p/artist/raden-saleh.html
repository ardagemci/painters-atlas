Raden Saleh

c. 1811–1880 · He learned the European history picture, then turned it on the people who taught him

Born near Semarang in Java into a noble Hadhrami family whose ancestors had reached the island from Arabia by way of Surat.

The animal fights made his name — a lion tamer in The Hague named Henri Martin let him study a live lion, and the results, all teeth and rearing horses, sold across Europe as Delacroix's subject matter done properly.
