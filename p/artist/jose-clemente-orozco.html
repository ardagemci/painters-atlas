José Clemente Orozco

1883–1949 · The muralist who refused to make the revolution look good

He lost his left hand and much of his hearing to a childhood accident with gunpowder, and came to painting through architectural drawing and political cartooning.

With Diego Rivera and David Alfaro Siqueiros he made the Mexican mural renaissance, and he is the one who complicates it.
