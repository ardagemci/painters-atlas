Robert Campin

c. 1378–1444 · Not one signed picture — and early Netherlandish realism begins with him

He was settled in Tournai by 1405–06 as a free master of the guild of goldsmiths and painters, possibly having come from Valenciennes.

No surviving painting can be tied to him by a signature or a contemporary document.
