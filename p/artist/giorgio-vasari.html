Giorgio Vasari

1511–1574 · He invented art history, then wrote himself into the middle of it

Born in Arezzo and trained in Florence, Vasari made himself indispensable to the Medici — effectively the court's minister of culture, running painting, architecture and pageantry for a state that understood all three as politics.

His Lives of the Most Excellent Painters, Sculptors and Architects (1550, expanded 1568) is the foundation of Western art history — the first attempt to treat art as a story with a direction, told through the biographies of the people who made it.
