Alphonse Mucha

1860–1939 · Sarah Bernhardt's poster artist — who spent his second life on the Slav Epic

He was born in 1860 in Ivančice in Moravia, a court usher's son, and sang in a Brno church choir to pay for school.

At the end of 1894 Sarah Bernhardt ordered a new poster in a hurry for her play Gismonda, and Mucha, who was at the printer's correcting proofs, got it.
