Basawan

fl. 1580–1600 · Akbar's greatest painter of people, and one of four the chronicle bothered to name

A Yadav from Uttar Pradesh who came into Akbar's workshop and rose under the Persian master Abd al-Samad.

Where the workshop's Persian inheritance made faces into pattern, Basawan made them into people: a crowd in an Akbarnama page has individuals in it, watching different things, and reacting at different speeds.
