Pigment · an atlas of painters

Robert Motherwell

1915–1991 · One elegy, painted a hundred and seventy times

The son of a bank president, asthmatic and bookish, he studied philosophy at Stanford and Harvard and art history at Columbia, where Meyer Schapiro told him to stop writing about painting and go paint.

From 1948 until his death he returned to a single image: heavy black ovoids clamped between vertical bars, titled Elegy to the Spanish Republic and numbered past 170.
