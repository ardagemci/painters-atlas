Filippo Lippi

c. 1406–1469 · The Carmelite friar who painted tender Madonnas — and ran off with his model

He was born in Florence in 1406, a butcher's son, orphaned at two and placed with the Carmelites of Santa Maria del Carmine at eight.

The Carmine's chapel had just been frescoed by Masaccio, and the young friar went there so often that, Vasari says, people claimed Masaccio's spirit had entered him.
