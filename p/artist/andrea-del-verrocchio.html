Andrea del Verrocchio

c. 1435–1488 · The workshop that produced Leonardo — and barely signed anything

He was born Andrea di Michele di Francesco de' Cioni and took the name Verrocchio from the goldsmith he trained under — a working man's inheritance rather than a family one.

Verrocchio is the rare master whose importance is easier to prove through other people's work than his own.
