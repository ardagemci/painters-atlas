Paolo Uccello

1397–1475 · Up all night with the vanishing point — and a battle of toy-bright horses

Paolo di Dono was born in 1397, probably at Pratovecchio, the son of a barber-surgeon; the nickname Uccello, "little bird", came from his fondness for painting birds.

Vasari's Uccello is obsessed with perspective, up all night in his study trying to fix the vanishing point, and his Annunciation for Santa Maria Maggiore became a model for painters who wanted illusions of space.
