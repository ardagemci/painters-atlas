Jean-Léon Gérôme

1824–1904 · The most famous painter alive in 1880, and the case against his own genre

Gérôme trained under Paul Delaroche and rose fast inside the French academic system, which he then largely ran: Salon medals, a professorship at the École des Beaux-Arts, and a teaching career long enough to touch several generations.

His subjects were history, Greek myth, portraiture and the Near East, all painted with a finish so smooth it reads as photographic — the surface that made him a fortune in prints and made the Impressionists treat him as the enemy.
