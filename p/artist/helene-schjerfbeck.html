Helene Schjerfbeck

1862–1946 · The face stays — the paint learns how much it can leave out

Born in Helsinki in 1862, Schjerfbeck injured her hip in a childhood fall and lived with a lasting limp.

Her early history pictures gave way to portraits and still lifes, with description gradually pared towards abstraction.
