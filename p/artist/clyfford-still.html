Clyfford Still

1904–1980 · Jagged fields, and a lifelong exit

He grew up working wheat on the northern plains — North Dakota, Alberta, eastern Washington — and the labour never left his painting: hard weather, hard ground, nothing decorative.

His canvases are vast vertical fields torn by ragged edges, laid on thick with a palette knife, as if the surface had split to show something behind it.
