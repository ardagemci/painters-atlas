Gwen John

1876–1939 · Hands in laps, colours close together — a room for looking slowly

Gwen John was born in Haverfordwest in 1876 and grew up sketching on the coast at Tenby.

Her portraits bring closely related tones around a seated woman, often with hands resting in her lap.
