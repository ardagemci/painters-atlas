Melchor Pérez de Holguín

c. 1660–c. 1735 · Potosí's Golden Brush — dark tones give way to colour

Holguín was born around 1660 in Cochabamba and made his career in Potosí, in what is now Bolivia.

Religious orders supplied most of his commissions, with wealthy landowners also among his patrons.
