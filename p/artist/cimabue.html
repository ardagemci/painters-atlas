Cimabue

c. 1240–1302 · The first Italian to loosen the Byzantine icon — then eclipsed by Giotto

Cenni di Pepo was born in Florence around 1240 and died in Pisa around 1302, and little else about his life is certain.

He trained on Byzantine models and was among the first Italian painters to break from them, with more lifelike proportions and shading that suggests volume.
