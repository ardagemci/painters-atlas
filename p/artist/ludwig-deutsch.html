Ludwig Deutsch

1855–1935 · Egypt assembled in a Paris studio, down to the last tile

Born in Vienna and trained at its Academy from 1872, he moved to Paris in 1878 and joined the circle of Orientalist painters working there.

Deutsch is the clearest case of how the genre actually worked.
