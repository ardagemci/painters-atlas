Miguel Cabrera

1695–1768 · The most famous painter in the Americas, and Europe never heard of him

Born at Nueva Antequera, now Oaxaca, and in Mexico City from 1719.

His portrait of Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz — the nun, poet and scholar who had died fifty years before he painted her — is the image by which she is known everywhere, a woman at a desk in front of her own library, which was the argument.
