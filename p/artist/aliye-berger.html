Aliye Berger

1903–1974 · One of Turkey's first engravers, who started at forty-four

She was born into the remarkable Şakir Pasha family — her sister was the painter Fahrelnissa Zeid, her brother the writer known as the Fisherman of Halicarnassus.

She became one of the first engravers in Turkey, and the expressionist bite of the prints — Istanbul rooftops, boats, crowds, all cut in restless line — is the reason the medium took hold there when it did.
