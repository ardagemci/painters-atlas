Joaquín Torres-García

1874–1949 · South America turns upside down — the compass becomes an argument

Torres-García was born in Montevideo in 1874, the son of a Catalan shopkeeper and a Uruguayan mother.

His mature paintings place simplified figures and signs inside a geometric structure, holding representation and abstraction together.
