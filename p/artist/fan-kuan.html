Fan Kuan

c. 960–c. 1030 · He put a cliff where the subject should be, and left the people out of focus

Born Fan Zhongzheng, known by the nickname Kuan — 'generous', or 'easy-going' — which is most of what the record preserves of his character.

Travelers among Mountains and Streams is probably the only picture by him that survives, and it is enough.
