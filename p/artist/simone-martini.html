Simone Martini

c. 1284–1344 · Siena's most elegant line — carried from the council hall to the papal court

He was born in Siena around 1284, and very little documentation of his life survives.

Among his first documented works is the Maestà of 1315 in Siena's Palazzo Pubblico, whose model other painters followed for the rest of the century.
