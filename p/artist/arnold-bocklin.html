Arnold Böcklin

1827–1901 · A rowing boat, a white figure, a cypress island — a dream everyone borrowed

He was born in Basel in 1827, the son of a silk merchant, and trained at the Düsseldorf academy under Schirmer, who sent him to copy the Flemish and Dutch masters in Antwerp and Brussels.

Rome turned a landscape painter into a painter of myths: nymphs and satyrs, centaurs and sea idylls, set among classical architecture and often haunted by death.
