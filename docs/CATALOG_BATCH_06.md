# Catalog Batch 06 — the last run of inbound gravity

*2026-08-24. Backlog **E1**. Twelve records, `js/catalog-9.js`. Reviewed by the
Curator before selection; his judgement is recorded here and was followed.*

---

## The rule retires with this batch

The Curator's diagnosis, and it is the right one: **the measure has completed,
not failed.** Inbound gravity asks one question — *what does the atlas lean on
and fail to show* — and three batches drained the painters it leans on hard. The
top score is now 9 where Batch 03's was 28, and a 9 against an 8 is **one prose
mention**. At that resolution the ranking is measuring paragraph length, not
significance.

That licenses a specific action rather than a vague one: **ship this batch on
the rule, and retire it in the same document.** A fourth flat band would be an
arbitrary cut wearing the costume of a reproducible one.

**Do not read the boundary landing on exactly twelve as validation.** The 9-band
(seven) plus the 8-band (five) came to twelve, as the 11+10 bands did last time.
Batch 05 called that luck. It is luck again.

### The successor rule, named

**Outbound absence.** Rank uncatalogued public-domain painters by *(their
tradition's catalog-record count, ascending)* × *(audited images held)*, with a
floor requiring an opened, verified hero image. It asks what a visitor can **see**
rather than what the prose **mentions**, and it cannot flatten, because the
tradition counts are integers far apart — Song now 2 records, Mughal 1, Joseon 0.

It is not gap-filling in the sense the curator brief forbids: nothing new is
admitted to the atlas, only the order of work on painters already in it changes.

## The signal is partly endogenous, and Batch 05 understated it

Four of these twelve — **Fan Kuan, Shen Zhou, Hasegawa Tōhaku, Abd al-Samad** —
are painters the **E3 roster pass added days ago**, and two of them score partly
on influence edges *that same pass drew*: `guo-xi → fan-kuan`, and the
`behzad → abd-al-samad → basawan` chain.

Batch 05 wrote "nothing was pushed". The Curator's correction: **true about
intent, false about independence.** The same team authored the numerator, and a
fourth batch repeating the phrase would be the atlas grading its own homework in
public.

The remedy is disclosure, not exclusion, because **a tradition with a painter
page and no work page is the worse error by a wide margin** — it is visible to
every visitor, where a soft ranking is visible to nobody. So all four ship, and
the record is: **selected on our own signal, admitted on external grounds.**
*Travelers Among Mountains and Streams*, *Pine Trees*, *Lofty Mount Lu* and the
Akbari workshop stand on the literature, not on our edge count.

**The Curator also set a condition, and it was checked.** E3 records that Fan
Kuan's image first resolved to a signature crop with a red annotation box, and
that a Mansur file turned out to be a museum wall label — so he asked that Fan
Kuan and Abd al-Samad's pool images be **opened before either was scheduled**.
Both were. Fan Kuan's is the Google Art Project scan of the whole scroll
(substituted during E3); Abd al-Samad's is the British Museum's cloth painting,
damaged, which is the object's real condition and not a bad file.

## §8 stays exactly as it is

Tier 1 has been frozen at 75 while four batches added 48 Tier 2 records, and the
question was whether §8's inbound-link rule is what starves the deck.

**It is not, and the measurement settles it.** Of every painter who died by 1955,
holds audited images and lacks a catalog record, **exactly one is genuinely
abstract** (Lyubov Popova). Which means the abstract painters — Kandinsky,
Malevich, Mondrian, Klee, af Klint — are *already in the atlas and already
catalogued*. Their works are not reaching the deck because they have passed
through none of §8's four doors, not because the doors are too narrow. Relaxing
§8 would admit orphan Tier 1 pages and produce **not one additional abstract
work**.

The honest route is **door 1: an editorial list on abstraction before 1955** —
the same route the Vasari exclusion pointed *Six Tuscan Poets* down. A list
commission plus coordinate work on records that already exist; no schema change.
Filed in the backlog.

## Four museum notes, and five bad photographs

Three venues were opened for the works — **British Museum**, **National Museum in
Poznań**, **National Museum in Kraków** — and the validator caught a fourth:
`sakip-sabanci` was **already in the venue registry with no museum note**, so
filing Şeker Ahmed Paşa's *Forest* there tripped *"venue holds works but has no
museum note"*. Exactly what that rule exists for.

Photograph hunting for the two Polish museums produced **five consecutive
unusable files**: a sgraffito frieze panel, a roofline parapet, a street scene
dominated by a watch-shop window, a wall sign reading *Sztuka Polska XX wieku*,
and a logo. Each museum's **own Wikipedia lead image** then gave a proper
building. Every photograph in this batch was opened before use.

## Dimensions

Two records carry **no `dims`**, and both are deliberate:

- `princes-of-the-house-of-timur` — Wikidata's search returns **the wrong
  object** for this title: an 18th-century Bodleian album page, not the British
  Museum cloth painting. Nothing was taken from it.
- `motherhood-wyspianski` — two published sizes (58.8 × 91 and 60 × 93 cm) and
  neither clearly the holder's. Recording a coin-flip would be worse than
  recording nothing.

`pine-trees-tohaku` carries **"Each screen 156.8 × 356 cm"** — the §7.1 rule-3
case (a pair of screens), handled the way the schema prescribes: a hand-authored
string that says what is being measured.
